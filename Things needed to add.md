> This file is kept as the original idea list.

Here is a comprehensive list of features, improvements, and integrations that can be added to the Vajra Fitness Gym Management System to make it a more robust and complete SaaS product:

## 1. Member Experience & Mobile App
- **Dedicated Mobile App (React Native/Flutter)**: A dedicated app for members to view their workout plans, book PT sessions, and track their attendance.
- **Biometric/QR Code Check-in**: Integrate with physical turnstiles or provide a QR code in the app for quick check-ins.
- **Diet & Nutrition Plans**: Allow trainers to create and assign custom diet plans to members.
- **Goal Tracking & Progress Photos**: Members can upload progress photos and track body measurements (weight, BMI, body fat percentage).
- **Gamification & Leaderboards**: Points for attending classes, completing workouts, or hitting PRs.
- **Wearable Integration**: Sync with Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, or Garmin to track out-of-gym activities.

## 2. Payments & Billing
- **Recurring Subscriptions (Auto-Pay)**: e-Mandate or Stripe/Razorpay auto-debit for monthly memberships.
- **Point of Sale (POS) System**: For selling supplements, merchandise, and water bottles at the front desk.
- **Wallet/Credit System**: Members can load money into a digital wallet to buy smoothies, PT sessions, etc.
- **Expense Tracking**: Gym owners can log electricity, rent, and maintenance expenses to calculate net profit.

## 3. Marketing & Retention
- **WhatsApp/SMS Automation**: Automated messages for membership expiry, birthday wishes, and missed attendance.
- **Referral Program**: Automated discounts or free months for members who bring in new signups.
- **Lead Generation/CRM**: Integration with Facebook/Instagram lead ads to automatically capture and track prospective members.
- **Push Notifications**: For flash sales, class cancellations, or schedule changes.

## 4. Analytics & Reporting
- **Advanced Dashboard Graphs**: Churn rate, Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
- **Trainer Performance Reports**: Revenue generated by each trainer through PT sessions, member retention rates per trainer.
- **Peak Hours Analysis**: Heatmaps showing the busiest hours and days to optimize staff scheduling.
- **Automated Email Reports**: Weekly/monthly performance summaries emailed directly to gym owners.

## 5. Operations & Facilities
- **Equipment Maintenance Tracker**: Log equipment issues, track repair costs, and set maintenance schedules.
- **Class/Group Fitness Management**: Scheduling and booking for Zumba, Yoga, CrossFit classes with capacity limits.
- **Staff Payroll Management**: Calculate salary based on fixed pay + commission per PT session.
- **Multi-Language Support**: Allow staff and members to switch between English, Hindi, and other regional languages.
- **Inventory Management**: Track stock levels for retail items (protein powder, t-shirts) with low-stock alerts.
