# Brand Identity Proposals

This directory contains three distinct, high-end branding concepts created for the gym management software platform. Each direction is non-AI generic, crafted with high-resolution vector assets, specific color palettes, and market positioning.

---

## 1. Kavach Fit (कवच)
> **Tagline:** The Indestructible Gym Operating System  
> **Brand Persona:** Resilient, Indian Heritage + Modern Tech, Authoritative, Unbreakable.  
> **Asset:** [`branding/kavach-logo.svg`](./kavach-logo.svg)

- **Rationale:** "Kavach" (Sanskrit for Armor / Shield) conveys rock-solid financial security, reliable attendance tracking, and unbreakable infrastructure for Indian gym owners.
- **Palette:**
  - Primary Crimson: `#E63946`
  - Deep Burgundy: `#9D0208`
  - Dark Charcoal Shield: `#1F2421`
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
- **Typography:** Outfit (Extra Bold / Heavy 900) paired with Inter for data density.
- **Best Suited For:** Gym owners looking for trust, high reliability, and a strong Indian identity that rivals global brands.

---

## 2. Kratos OS
> **Tagline:** Performance Gym Infrastructure  
> **Brand Persona:** Raw power, Spartan discipline, high velocity, minimalist dark-mode software.  
> **Asset:** [`branding/kratos-logo.svg`](./kratos-logo.svg)

- **Rationale:** Named after the deity of strength and power. Stripped of fluff, built for performance bodybuilding facilities, CrossFit boxes, and modern strength gyms.
- **Palette:**
  - Electric Ember: `#FF5400`
  - Neon Crimson: `#FF0054`
  - Pitch Onyx: `#0B090A`
  - Border Steel: `#161A1D`
- **Typography:** Bebas Neue for display headings + Outfit / Space Grotesk for metrics.
- **Best Suited For:** Hardcore strength clubs, modern athletic complexes, and fast-growing franchise chains.

---

## 3. ApexForge
> **Tagline:** Intelligent Gym Suite  
> **Brand Persona:** Precision engineering, modern enterprise SaaS, clean, data-driven.  
> **Asset:** [`branding/apexforge-logo.svg`](./apexforge-logo.svg)

- **Rationale:** "Apex" (the peak of fitness performance) + "Forge" (where champions and successful gym businesses are shaped). Clean, Silicon Valley / Linear style aesthetic.
- **Palette:**
  - Cobalt Pulse: `#0066FF`
  - Electric Cyan: `#00D2FF`
  - Deep Slate: `#0F172A`
  - Muted Steel: `#64748B`
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter with geometric proportions.
- **Best Suited For:** Premium wellness clubs, boutique studios, and multi-location commercial fitness chains.

---

## How to Apply a Brand Direction

To activate any of these brands:
1. Copy the selected SVG logo to `frontend/public/logo.svg`.
2. Update the brand name in `frontend/src/components/Navbar.tsx` and `MainLayout.tsx`.
3. Adjust root CSS color variables in `frontend/src/index.css` according to the palette above.
